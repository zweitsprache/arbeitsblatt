Exercise 02 · The mark before the message

Three marks.
One v. Three ways to make it earn its place.

Each proposal keeps the established system — Encode Sans, steel blue, signal coral — and rebuilds the wordmark around a different idea of what the final letter is doing. None use italics; sophistication comes from construction, not slant.

A / 03Vektor
vektorgeometric · arrow

The final v is redrawn as a precise geometric chevron — sharper than the typeface's native v, and slightly longer on the right diagonal so it reads as a vector pointing forward. The brand's pedagogical metaphor (direction) becomes the mark.

Behaviour

At sizes below 24 px the right stroke shortens to cap height — the arrow flavour is a luxury reserved for hero and editorial use. The mark never tilts, never animates, never becomes literal.

  • Custom letter — uncopyable

02Where it bends

03Best fit

Product surfaces, app icons, motion. The arrow shape rewards motion — a 200 ms ease-out as it draws in on first paint would feel native, but is optional.

B / 03Annotat
annotat★ recommended

The wordmark is left structurally untouched — same Encode Sans, same width, no custom letters. A precise signal hairline sits beneath the v, exactly v-width, like a teacher's red underline. A small v. footnote tag annotates the wordmark as a dictionary entry would. Sophistication through restraint.

Two-tier system

Full mark with v. 1. tag for hero use. Stripped mark (rule + word only) for chrome, favicon, business systems. Both share one master.

C / 03Konstrukt
konstruktmonogram-led

A primary logomark — not a wordmark. A constructed square monogram where the lowercase d and v share strokes: the d's vertical stem is the v's left diagonal extended; the v sits inside the d's bowl. Pure geometric Swiss construction. The full wordmark becomes a descriptor lockup.

Lockup logic

Monogram is always primary. Wordmark appears as a small spaced-out descriptor (tracking +0.22 em) beneath or beside — never larger than 0.25× the mark.
