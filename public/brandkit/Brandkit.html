Mix rule · 60 / 30 / 8 / 2

Paper at 60. Steel + Ink at 30. Greys at 8. Signal capped at 2 percent of any surface. If you can see two signals at once, one of them is wrong.

Contrast minimum

Body text against Paper: Ink or Grey 700 only. Steel 500 and lighter never carry copy below 14 px. Signal never used for long-form text — only headlines or single words.

Print conversion

Pantone match: Steel 700 ≈ 7546 C · Signal ≈ 7416 C. For uncoated stock, drop one step lighter to retain ink saturation under classroom lamp light.

04 / 07Grid & spacing

Twelve columns. Eight steps.
The page is a piece of squared paper.

All layouts sit on a 12-column grid with a 24 px gutter, atop an 8 px baseline. Spacing tokens are geometric, not arbitrary — every step doubles or halves the one before it, so density choices are explicit, not improvised.

Iconography rule

We draw with rectangles. Every icon must fit on a 4 × 4 grid using only solid blocks, hairlines, and the directional arrow. No outlined glyphs, no rounded corners, no decoration.

06 / 07Voice

Speak like a good teacher.
Not a good salesman.

We write declaratively — short sentences, no euphemism, one idea per paragraph. We make space for the student to think. We never say simply, we never say just, and we never say amazing.

✓
Do

"Three exercises today. Twelve minutes. We'll look at fractions."

Why

Specific, measured, sets a contract the student can hold us to.

✕
Don't

"Get ready for an exciting new adventure in mathematics!"

Why not

Excitement is the student's job. Ours is to be clear.

✓
Do

"You got six of ten. That's enough to continue. The four we missed are on page 14."

Why

Honest result, honest next step, no false praise.

✕
Don't

"Wow, great effort! Keep going, you've totally got this 🎉"

Why not

Confetti doesn't teach. And the emoji is louder than the message.

✓
Do

"This is hard. Most students need three tries. You're on try two."

Why

Names the difficulty, normalises struggle, gives an honest landmark.

A lesson is not an experience to be had. It is a question to be finished.
